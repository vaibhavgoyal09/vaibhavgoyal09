### 🚀 About Me

I am the Founder of **VoluChat**, a SaaS platform dedicated to revolutionizing how small and medium-sized businesses manage sales and automation across social channels. My mission is to bridge the gap between AI and human interaction, enabling merchants to scale their operations on WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook through intelligent automation.

- 🏗️ **Building**: [VoluChat](https://voluchat.com) — The AI-powered commerce engine for social sellers.
- 💡 **Expertise**: Scalable Microservices, AI/LLM Integrations, and Omnichannel Automation.
- 🛠️ **Tech Focus**: NestJS, React, Redis, OpenAI, and AWS.
- 🌍 **Mission**: Empowering 1M+ Indian SMEs with bespoke automation tools.

---

### 📦 What is VoluChat?

**VoluChat** is an all-in-one business automation suite designed for the modern social merchant. We help businesses turn their DMs into a high-converting sales machine.

- **🤖 AI Shopping Agents**: Personalized shopping assistants that handle inquiries, product recommendations, and checkouts.
- **📱 Omnichannel Sync**: Unified management of conversations across WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook.
- **⚡ Automated Workflows**: From lead capture to order fulfillment, we automate the repetitive so you can focus on growth.
- **🎯 Bespoke Integrations**: Custom AI models tailored to specific niches like Fashion, Beauty, and Footwear.
